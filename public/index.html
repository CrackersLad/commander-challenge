    📜 How to Play & Draft Rules
    1
    Setup & Rules: The Host configures budgets (Commander & Deck), EDHREC Rank boundaries, power bracket limit, and format. Add a Discord Webhook for notifications.
    2
    Draft Your Commander: Roll random options, utilize your allowed slot rerolls, or manually search. Enable Blind Draft to keep picks secret until everyone locks in!
    3
    Build & Seal: Brew on Moxfield or Archidekt. We verify 100-card legality, price, salt score, and power bracket. Lock in when Battle Ready.
    4
    Schedule & Battle: Use the Availability Calendar to pick a match time. The Host locks in the date, prize, and format.
    5
    Crown the Victor: The Host clicks Declare Winner to award lifetime wins to the champion's profile and reset the lobby for the next challenge!
