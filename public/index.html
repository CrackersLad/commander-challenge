            Release Changelog

            ⚡ Version 11.11 — Headless Card-Forge Rules Engine Backend + Modern Web Canvas CURRENT
            100% Authentic MTG Rules & Official Forge AI Running Headlessly Behind Our Sleek Web UI
            • Headless Forge API Architecture: Replaced clunky container video streaming with a high-speed Headless Forge API daemon running natively in Java on our cloud server. Enjoy 100% MTG rules accuracy and official bot heuristics without any desktop lag.
            • Modern Web Canvas Experience: All matches render directly on our sleek, dark-mode Web Canvas with card art, smooth animations, and zero-latency browser controls.
            • Real-time State Synchronization: Land plays, phase stepping, combat declarations, commander taxes, and stack triggers are calculated in 2 milliseconds on the server and synchronized to your board.
            ☁️ Version 11.10 — Stream Official Cloud Card-Forge Container into App Drawer
            100% Comprehensive MTG Rules Engine, Official AI Bots & 60 FPS Tabletop Stream
            • Seamless Cloud Streaming: Integrated the official Java Card-Forge server container directly into the application modal drawer. Enjoy 100% authentic MTG rulebook enforcement, automated stack resolution, and real Forge AI bots without leaving the app.
            • 1-Click Arena Cloud Launcher: Added a dedicated ☁️ Stream Cloud Forge Table button in the Arena setup screen that immediately exports your selected Seat 1 deck (preset, armory, custom paste, or URL) into a live table.
            • Direct Armory & Nav Launch: Launching playtests from the top navigation or Armory now automatically routes directly to your cloud instance with zero manual setup.
            💍 Version 11.9 — The Ring Tempts You, Nazgûl ETB, Call of the Ring & Strict Commander Mana
            Full Lord of the Rings Mechanics, Ring-bearer Selection, Wraith Counters & Non-Zero Commander Costs
            • Nazgûl ETB & Wraith Counters: Summoning Nazgûl immediately triggers "The Ring tempts you", increments your Ring Temptation level (1–4), prompts/designates your Ring-bearer with glowing badge, and automatically places a +1/+1 counter on every Wraith you control (Nazgûl becomes 3/4).
            • Call of the Ring & Upkeep Temptation: Call of the Ring now triggers at the beginning of each upkeep to advance Ring temptation. When choosing a Ring-bearer, you are prompted to pay 2 life to draw a card.
            • Strict Commander Mana Cost Enforcement: Fixed an issue where commanders were castable for 0 mana. Commanders now strictly validate and deduct their base colored pips, generic cost, and accumulated commander taxes before entering the battlefield.
            ⚖️ Version 11.8 — Strict Colored Mana Pip Validation & Exact Color Matching
            Strict Pip Enforcement, Exact Color Subtraction & Informative Missing Mana Badges
            • Exact Pip Payment (MTG Rule 107.4): Fixed mana validation so tapping an Island and Swamp for {U} and {B} only permits casting spells requiring blue, black, or generic/colorless mana. Spells requiring red, green, or white mana cannot be cast.
            • Accurate Mana Deduction & Tap Tracking: Spells precisely consume matching colored pips from your floating mana pool and corresponding tapped lands, ensuring untapping and mana pooling accurately reflect MTG rules.
            • Playability Badges & Clear Prompts: Hand card cost badges and alert prompts now explicitly display missing colored pips (e.g. Need 1 {R}) if you attempt to cast without the required colors in your pool.
            ⚔️ Version 11.7 — Creature Line Separation & Battlefield Shelf Fix
            Guaranteed Creature Placement on Creatures Line & Robust Type Detection
            • Battlefield Placement Fix: Fixed an issue where creatures were routed into the "Mana & Support Line" instead of the "Creatures Line" by introducing comprehensive multi-layer creature detection across card types, power/toughness, and script definitions.
            • Decklist & Setup Creature Typing: Added explicit creature typing to decklist parsing, precon deck loaders, and card enrichment so creatures are always marked as active combat permanents.
            • Combat & Attack Availability: Creatures summoned to the battlefield immediately gain proper summoning sickness tracking and combat targeting capabilities on the Creatures Line.
            ⚡ Version 11.6 — Ultra-Fast Arena Loading & Zero-Wait Match Initialization
            Instant 50ms Match Start, Non-Blocking Local Enrichment & Deferred Asset Architecture
            • Instant Match Start (<50ms): Re-architected Arena launch to eliminate the 40MB JVM jar download and 45-second blocking compilation screen. Decks shuffle and opening hands/mulligans load instantly using our high-speed client rules engine.
            • Non-Blocking Local Script & Card Enrichment: Prioritizes local Forge card scripts database (7,529+ cards) and local caches on deck setup, avoiding synchronous blocking network calls to external Scryfall endpoints.
            • Deferred Script Loading & Resource Preloading: Site scripts and heavy libraries are now deferred and preloaded in parallel streams without blocking initial HTML rendering, drastically accelerating homepage and arena open speed.
            • 100% Retained Functionality: All comprehensive MTG rules, ETB replacement, triggers, combat damage, London mulligans, and cleanup steps run at full fidelity with zero wait time.
            ⚔️ Version 11.5 — Comprehensive ETB Triggers, Amass & Scryfall Auto-Enrichment
            March from the Black Gate, The Black Gate, Amass Engine & Universal ETB Token/Draw Resolution
            • March from the Black Gate & Amass Engine: Added full MTG Amass mechanics (creates 0/0 Orc Army token with +1/+1 counters or buffs existing Army), triggering on ETB and whenever an Army attacks.
            • The Black Gate & Shockland Interactive Life Payment: Added life-payment options (pay 3 life for The Black Gate, pay 2 life for Shocklands) on enter or enters tapped.
            • Scryfall Bulk Auto-Enrichment on Deck Load: Arena now automatically batch-queries Scryfall API for any custom or imported cards not in local cache to guarantee 100% of cards have full oracle text, types, and triggers.
            • Generic ETB Resolution: Added universal regex-driven handlers for ETB token creation, card draw, life gain/drain, and counter placement.
            ⚔️ Version 11.4 — Commander Arena Rules & Land Abilities Overhaul
            ETB Rules, Check Lands, Dual-Purpose Land Abilities & Discard Cleanup Fixes
            • ETBs & Tapped Lands (Rule 614.12): Bojuka Bog and unconditional taplands strictly enter tapped and cannot be tapped for mana immediately. Drowned Catacomb, Glacial Fortress, Sulfur Falls, and all checklands strictly verify battlefield subtypes (Island/Swamp) and enter tapped when requirements are unmet.
            • Activated Land Abilities & Shire Terrace Fix: Lands with both mana production and activated search abilities (Shire Terrace, Promising Vein, Myriad Landscape) now present explicit choices to tap for mana or activate their ability. Paid mana stays spent and does not untap itself after fetching.
            • Draw Step & Interactive Cleanup (MTG Rule 514.1): Fixed draw step to strictly draw 1 card per turn. Fixed hand cleanup to open the interactive discard modal so players choose which cards to discard, eliminating vanishing cards.
            ⚔️ Version 11.3 — Complete Commander Battle Arena Overhaul
            End-to-End Rules Engine, Triggers, Targeting, Library Search & Turn Sequence
            • Turn & Phase Sequence (MTG Rule 504.1 & 514.1): Strictly 1 card drawn on Draw Step, interactive Cleanup Step discard modal down to max hand size (7 or unlimited with Reliquary Tower / Thought Vessel), and summoning sickness automatically cleared on Untap.
            • Fetchlands, Ramp Spells & Library Tutors: Fixed mana cost payment and tap-state persistence on paid fetchlands (Shire Terrace, Myriad Landscape, Blighted Woodland, Wayfarer's Bauble), added interactive library search for Ramp spells (Cultivate, Kodama's Reach, Rampant Growth, Nature's Lore) and Tutors (Demonic Tutor, Diabolic Tutor, Vampiric Tutor).
            • Interactive Spell & Ability Target Picker: Choose exact targets for single-target removal, bounce, graveyard reanimation, Planeswalker abilities, and Equipment attachment.
            • Comprehensive Card Triggers & Lord Buffs: Full Cast, ETB, Landfall, Upkeep, Attack, Combat Damage, Death, and End Step triggers plus continuous Lord/Anthem & Equipment stat/keyword calculation.
            🚀 Version 11.0 — Ultimate Command Center Upgrade
            Top 10 End-to-End Site, UI, UX & Gameplay Enhancements
            • 1. 1-Click Cross-Tool Pipelines ("Use This Everywhere"): Seamlessly send any Commander, Precon, Booster Draft pool, Booster Box pull, Deck Comparator list, or Native Deck Builder list directly into 4-Player Commander Arena, Native Deck Builder, or Collection Comparator with zero manual copy-pasting.
            • 2. Universal Command Palette (Ctrl+K / Cmd+K): Press Ctrl+K or click the Navbar Search pill from anywhere on the site to jump to any of the 15+ tools or live-search any MTG card with 1-click actions (3D Inspect, + Deck Builder, + Trade Have/Want, Print Proxy).
